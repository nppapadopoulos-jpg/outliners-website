Privacy Policy.

Prepared for final approval before submission forms are enabled.

Controller and contact

OutLiners, operating in Athens, Greece, manages submissions on this website. For privacy enquiries and rights requests, contact info@outliners.gr. The controller's full legal identity will be confirmed before the forms open.

Data, purposes and legal bases

The CV form collects contact details, specialization, preferred location, the CV and optional information you choose to provide. We use these to consider and discuss relevant career opportunities with you, based on your consent. Health information, identity document numbers and other sensitive information are not required.

The hiring request form collects business contact details, company and hiring needs. We use these to respond and discuss a potential engagement, based on our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries. Required fields are needed for this communication; other fields are optional.

Access and storage

Submissions are held privately in Microsoft Azure infrastructure in Europe. Only the authorized OutLiners Owner can access the Dashboard and files, using Microsoft Entra ID and MFA. Microsoft provides infrastructure as a processor under its contractual terms. Files have no public download links. CVs and submission details are not attached to notification emails.

This website does not automatically forward submissions to clients, rank candidates or make automated decisions. Any further disclosure for a particular opportunity will be discussed separately.

Retention

Each submission and its file are deleted after 12 calendar months, with the deletion check running hourly. Deleted objects remain recoverable by infrastructure administrators for up to seven additional days for error recovery, without Dashboard access. Temporary pseudonymized abuse-prevention data is pruned after 24 hours and the next hourly maintenance run.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and object to processing. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact info@outliners.gr. We normally respond within one month and will inform you if a permitted extension is needed. You may also contact the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Cookies and technical data

The website uses no advertising cookies or visitor tracking tools. Hosting and identity providers may process technical data and essential cookies for operation, security and sign-in. Dashboard data and tokens remain in the active session's memory, without localStorage or offline caching.
