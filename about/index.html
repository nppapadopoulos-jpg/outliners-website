About

A boutique partner,
by design.

OutLiners is an independent recruitment and talent advisory practice based in Athens, working with companies in Greece and internationally. Deliberately small, deliberately senior.

How we work

The person you meet
does the work.

We keep the number of engagements low enough that every client gets senior attention from first briefing to final decision. Nothing is handed down a chain of juniors, and nothing is automated that deserves human judgment. We combine market evidence with real conversations — and we’d rather lose an engagement than overstate what we can deliver.

What we stand for

Four things
we won’t trade.

Discretion

Searches, conversations and candidate details are handled with absolute confidentiality — on both sides of the table.

Substance over volume

A short list of right people beats a long list of possible ones. We optimise for signal, not activity.

Honesty

About the market, about timelines, about candidates — and about our own limits. You’ll always hear what we actually think.

Partnership

We measure success in long-term relationships, not transactions — we’d rather be the partner you call first than a vendor you call once.

For candidates

A discreet
conversation.

If you’re thinking about your next move — even quietly — we’re happy to talk in confidence. No pressure, no circulation of your CV without your explicit consent.
