# OUTLINERS.GR — Website Master Brief (v0.1)

Status: working draft, awaiting owner approval. Source: Outliners 2027–2029 business plan and current brand/business-card reference supplied by owner.

## Brand constraints
- Preserve existing **OutLiners** wordmark and nested-square symbol. Reference is a screenshot of a black front / white reverse business card; obtain original vector SVG/PDF or high-resolution transparent asset before production.
- Brand direction: monochrome black/white, restrained premium typography, high contrast, generous whitespace, subtle motion. Do not invent new logo or claim new brand colors are approved.
- No client logos, testimonials, results or credentials without owner permission and verification.

## Positioning
Boutique Recruitment & Talent Advisory Partner serving companies in Greece and internationally. Broaden beyond tech while retaining strong technology expertise. Three pillars:
1. Recruitment & Executive Search
2. Embedded Talent Partner (retainer-based outsourced talent acquisition)
3. Talent Mapping & HR Advisory

## Target audiences
- CEOs, founders, COOs, HR Directors and hiring managers at growing companies, especially 30–300 staff.
- Sectors: Technology & Digital, Business & Professional Services, Finance, Marketing & Commercial, and selective Hospitality & Premium Services.
- Secondary: candidates who want a discreet contact channel.

## Embedded Talent Partner (proposed commercial model, not public pricing without approval)
- Lite: €1,500/month, 12h, up to 2 active roles, min 3 months.
- Growth: €2,500/month, 25h, up to 3 active roles, min 6 months.

## Functional requirements
- Mobile-first, responsive, accessible keyboard navigation and WCAG-oriented contrast.
- Fast performance, SEO metadata, structured data where appropriate, sitemap, robots.txt.
- Working contact form with spam protection, consent and reliable delivery (backend/provider to be chosen).
- Privacy/cookie management compliant with actual trackers; no unnecessary analytics before consent.
- Easy content maintenance and clear deployment path; keep domain/DNS and secrets out of repository.
- Do not implement CV upload or automated candidate scoring in MVP.
