Who it’s for

For roles where
“good enough” isn’t.

We work with CEOs, founders, COOs, HR directors and hiring managers — typically at companies of 30 to 300 people — who need to fill a critical position and want a partner who treats it with the seriousness it deserves. The best candidates for these roles are rarely applying; they have to be found, approached and genuinely engaged.

What’s included

A complete search,
end to end.

  • Role definition & briefWe align on scope, success profile and market realities before any outreach begins.
  • Market research & targetingWe identify the companies and talent pools where the right people actually are.
  • Direct, discreet approachPersonal outreach that respects candidates, confidentiality and your employer brand.
  • Structured, human-led assessmentIn-depth interviews against agreed criteria — not keyword filters or volume CV forwarding.
  • Focused shortlist with insightA small number of genuinely relevant candidates, each with honest, evidence-based commentary.
  • Offer & onboarding supportGuidance through negotiation, acceptance and the first months in the role.
